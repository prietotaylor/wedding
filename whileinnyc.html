While in NYC

Here are our recommendations for things to eat, drink, see, and do while you are in town!

Williamsburg & Greenpoint

If you're staying in one of our hotel blocks, then your home base will be Williamsburg, Brooklyn, an area that is packed with bars and restaurants and which enjoys a beautiful view (and short train ride) across the river to Manhattan. All of these spots are walkable from the hotels, but you can also get around the Williamsburg / Greenpoint area by Citi bike (rented using the Lyft app) or by subway (the G line). Google Maps is the best resource to compare your transport options.

Where to eat:

  • Tonchin Brooklyn | 109 N 3rd St, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Our favorite Japanese spot in the city
  • Pierozek | 592 Manhattan Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11222 | Our favorite Polish spot in the city
  • L'Industrie Pizza - Williamsburg | 254 S 2nd St, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Amazing pizza, get the burrata on top. Can be a long line
  • MáLà Project Greenpoint | 603 Manhattan Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11222 | Build your own Sichuan dry pot. The "little spicy" sauce base is complex and addictive
  • Super Burrito | 320 Bedford Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Our casual go-to for mission-style burritos
  • Mission Sandwich Social | 326 Bedford Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Creative subs with great options for meat-eaters, vegetarians, and vegans
  • Fini Pizza | 305 Bedrord Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Super thin-crust NY pizze. The tomato slice and the shallot / pepper slice are favorites
  • Apollo Bagels | 133 N 7th St, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Sourdough bagels that have exploded in popularity for good reason
  • Lilia | 567 Union Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Fancier, modern Italian spot. Reserve in advance or walk in during off-hours
  • Bamonte's Restaurant | 32 Withers St, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Historic red-sauce Italian spot with great ambiance in the dining room
  • Lion's Milk | 104 Roebling St, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Turkish pastries and coffee
