While in NYC

Here are our recommendations for things to eat, drink, see, and do while you are in town!

Williamsburg & Greenpoint

If you're staying in one of our hotel blocks, then your home base will be Williamsburg, Brooklyn, an area that is packed with bars and restaurants and which enjoys a beautiful view (and short train ride) across the river to Manhattan.

Where to eat:

  • Tonchin Brooklyn | 109 N 3rd St, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Our favorite Japanese spot in the city
  • Pierozek | 592 Manhattan Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11222 | Our favorite Polish spot in the city
  • L'Industrie Pizza - Williamsburg | Amazing pizza, get the burrata on top. Can be a long line
  • MáLà Project
  • Super Burrito
  • Mission Sandwich Social
  • Fini Pizza
  • Apollo Bagels
  • Lilia
  • Bamonte's
  • Lion's Milk
