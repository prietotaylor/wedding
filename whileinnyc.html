While in NYC

Here are our personal recommendations for things to eat, drink, see, and do while you are in town. Don't hesitate to text us if you have questions as you are planning your trip!

Williamsburg & Greenpoint

If you're staying in one of our hotel blocks, then your home base will be Williamsburg, an area that is packed with bars and restaurants and which enjoys a beautiful view (and short train ride) across the river to Manhattan. All of the spots listed in this section are walkable from the hotels, but you can also get around the Williamsburg / Greenpoint area by calling a car via Uber or Lyft, by Citi bike (rented using the Lyft app) or by subway (the G or L line, depending on your start and end poitns). Google Maps is the best resource to compare your transport options.

Where to eat:

  • Tonchin Brooklyn | 109 N 3rd St, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Our favorite Japanese spot in the city. We love the edamame and cucumber starters, the ramen and the dipping noodles, and the shaved ice desserts
  • Pierozek | 592 Manhattan Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11222 | Our favorite Polish spot in the city. We love the pierogis, the borscht, the Kielbasa dish, and Zubrowka bison grass vodka
  • L'Industrie Pizza - Williamsburg | 254 S 2nd St, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Amazing pizza, get the burrata on top. Can be a long line
  • MáLà Project Greenpoint | 603 Manhattan Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11222 | Build your own Sichuan dry pot, plus lots of other amazing Sichuan Chinese dishes. The "little spicy" dry pot sauce base is complex and addictive
  • Super Burrito | 320 Bedford Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Our casual go-to for mission-style burritos
  • Mission Sandwich Social | 326 Bedford Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Creative subs with great options for meat-eaters, vegetarians, and vegans
  • Fini Pizza | 305 Bedford Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Super thin-crust NY pizza. The tomato slice and the shallot / pepper slice are favorites
  • Apollo Bagels | 133 N 7th St, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Sourdough bagels that have exploded in popularity for good reason
  • Lilia | 567 Union Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Fancier, modern Italian spot. Reserve in advance or walk in during off-hours
  • Bamonte's Restaurant | 32 Withers St, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Historic red-sauce Italian spot with great ambiance in the dining room
  • Lion's Milk | 104 Roebling St, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Turkish pastries and coffee

Where to drink:

  • Grimm Artisanal Ales | 990 Metropolitan Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Our favorite brewery, where we always go for Aaron's birthday
  • TALEA Beer Co - Williamsburg | 87 Richardson St, Brooklyn, NY 11211 | Another brewery that we love
  • The Gutter | 200 N 14th St, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | Dive bar with first come, first served bowling

Things to do:

  • Walk, run, or Citi bike over the Williamsburg Bridge | You can use Google Maps to find the route, but the pedestrian path entrance is on Bedford Ave between S 5th St and S 6th St
  • See a movie at Nitehawk Cinema | 136 Metropolitan Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11249
  • Take in the view at Domino Park | 15 River St, Brooklyn, NY 11249 | River-front green space with a beautiful view of Manhattan

Fort Greene & Downtown Brooklyn

We have lived together in the Fort Greene neighborhood in Brooklyn for the last couple years, and we love it! If you want to get a glimpse of a classic, brownstone Brooklyn neighborhood, it is worth the quick trip via subway (on the G train) or by car (Uber or Lyft). There are tons of fantastic restaurants, and it is a nice walk from Fort Greene down to the DUMBO waterfront.

Where to eat:

  • Sailor | 228 Dekalb Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11205 | Upscale modern American restaurant where everything is fabulous. For a somewhat seafood-themed restaurant, it has a surprising number of non-seafood and vegetarian options. Reserve in advance
  • Habana Outpost | 757 Fulton St, Brooklyn, NY 11217 | Casual Latin American spot with a festive patio that gets packed on summer days
  • Peck's | 455A Myrtle Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11205 | Counter service cafe with delicious breakfast sandwiches, coffee and matcha, and baked goods
  • Joe & Sal's Pizzeria | 353 Myrtle Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11205 | Our favorite pizza in the city. Just a perfect, classic NY slice
  • SUKH | 723 Fulton St, Brooklyn, NY 11217 | Sit-down Thai spot with a great vibe and sensational food. Generally good to reserve in advance, but you might get lucky walking in
  • DSK Brooklyn | 710 Fulton St, Brooklyn, NY 11217 | German beer hall with great pretzels and a laid-back indoor/outdoor vibe
  • Bittersweet | 180 Dekalb Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11205 | Our go-too coffee shop. The line can get long but moves quickly
  • Thea | 17 Greene Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11238 | Bakery and cafe with fantastic baked goods and prepared foods
  • Colonia Verde | 219 Dekalb Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11205 | Sit-down Latin American spot with Aaron's favorite roasted chicken in the city. Great for brunch or dinner
  • Oh Dumplings | 108 S Oxford St, Brooklyn, NY 11217 | Dependable and delicious counter-service Chinese spot. Kathryn adores the braised tofu (both the spicy sauce and the garlic sauce are great)
  • baba cool | 33 Lafayette Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11217 | All-day cafe with a great breakfast sandwich (the "MEC") and a fun backyard
  • Los Burritos Juarez | 354 Myrtle Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11205 | Trendy burrito spot with handmade tortillas (made with lard, but vegetarian tortillas are available)
  • LaRina Pastificio & Vino | 387 Myrtle Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11205 | Our go-to spot for a special sit-down Italian dinner. Everything is delicious and the backyard is magical

Things to do:

  • Fort Greene Park | Our beloved, beautiful neighborhood park, with a fun farmer's market and craft fair on Saturdays in the morning and early afternoon
  • Greenlight Bookstore | 686 Fulton St, Brooklyn, NY 11217 | Fantastic independent bookstore
  • Brooklyn Bridge Park | Beautiful waterfront area with lots of walking paths, with the most spectacular view of Manhattan. Great to just wander along the waterfront
  • Walk the Brooklyn Bridge | Popular for a reason! Enter the Brooklyn Bridge Promenade walking path from Downtown Brooklyn at the intersection of Tillary St and Brooklyn Bridge Blvd
  • Ride the ferry from DUMBO to Manhattan | Go to the Fulton Ferry Landing at 1 Water St and get any ticket you choose, for a scenic ride across the East River to Manhattan

East Village, Greenwich Village, & West Village

Kathryn lived in Greenwich Village and then the East Village in the early years of our relationship, not far from where Aaron grew up. Below is a selection of places where we spent a lot of time as we got to know each other.

Where to eat:

  • Spicy Moon West Village | 68 W 3rd St, New York, NC 10012 | This is an entirely plant-based Chinese spot that we wouldn't hesitate to bring any meat-eater to. It's just so good. Get the wontons in chili oil, the Salt & Pepper Style tofu, and/or the General Tso's tofu
  • Yellow Rose | 102 3rd Ave, New York, NY 10003 | Probably our favorite restaurant in the city, though it has gotten a bit too popular. Just a super fun and tasty Tex-Mex place, where everything is great. If you go for brunch, get the kolaches. And you can't miss with the queso, the bean & cheese taco, and the papas ranchera taco
  • Via Carota | 51 Grove St, New York, NY 10014 | The first fancy-ish dinner that we went to together. Consistently named one of the best restaurants in the city. Fantastic Italian food. Try to get a reservation, or walk in and prepare to wait a while
  • Veselka | 144 2nd Ave, New York, NY 10003 | A go-to favorite for pierogis and other Ukrainian food, open late. Many fun nights there
  • Smør | 441 E 12th St, New York, NY 10019 | Tasty Scandinavian all-day cafe
  • Fiaschetteria Pistoia | 647 E 11th St, New York, NY 10019 | Lovely, cozy Italian spot with handmade pastas
  • Emmy Squared Pizza: East Village | 83 1st Ave, New York, NY 10003 | Detroit-style rectangular pizza. Get the MVP, which has marinara, vodka sauce, and nut-free pesto
  • Bleecker St. Pizza | 69 7th Ave S, New York, NY 10014 | A solid NY slice that Aaron swears is better than Joe's (try both and decide for yourself)

Where to drink:

  • The Half Pint | 76 W 3rd St, New York, NY 10012 | Nothing special, but we had our very first date here :)
  • The Immigrant | 341 E 9th St, New York, NY 10003 | Very chill wine bar where we have spent many a night with friends
  • Local | 144 Sullivan St, New York, NY 10012 | Aaron's long-time favorite craft beer bar

Things to do:

  • Washington Square Park | On our first date, we agreed to meet at the southeast corner of Washington Square Park. Kathryn went to the southwest corner and swore she was in the right place. We ended up meeting in the middle
  • Forbidden Planet | 832 Broadway, New York, NY 10003 | Nerd heaven - Aaron grew up going here for collectibles, comic books, and board games
  • Play pool at Amsterdam Billiards | 110 E 11th St, New York, NY 10003 | Right near Kathryn's East Village apartment, an extensive pool hall
  • The New Museum | 235 Bowery, New York, NY 10002 | Recently revamped, a very modern museum with thought-provoking exhibitions
